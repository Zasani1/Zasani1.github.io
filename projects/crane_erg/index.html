Crane–Robot Cooperative Control Framework

Constrained Control with Explicit Reference Governor (ERG)

Home Publications 3D Simulation Contact

Abstract

This project presents a constrained control framework for cooperative manipulation of heavy payloads using a heterogeneous dual-robot system composed of a 7-DoF Franka Emika Panda manipulator and a 2-DoF overhead crane. The proposed approach integrates the Explicit Reference Governor (ERG) with a pre-stabilized control scheme to ensure constraint satisfaction without online optimization.

A trajectory-based ERG formulation, combining a Dynamic Safety Margin (DSM) and a Navigation Field (NF), dynamically adjusts the reference trajectory to guarantee safe and coordinated motion between the two robots. Experimental results confirm that the proposed scheme enables the manipulator to handle payloads several times heavier than its nominal capacity while maintaining safe, constraint-compliant cooperation.

Mathematical Model

The coupled dynamics of the crane–robot system are modeled as a closed-chain mechanical system using the Euler–Lagrange formalism. The resulting equations of motion are:

Equation (14):

\[ M(q)\ddot{q} + C(q,\dot{q})\dot{q} + g(q) = Su + A^T(q)\lambda \]

where \(M(q)\) is the inertia matrix, \(C(q,\dot{q})\) the Coriolis–centrifugal term, \(g(q)\) the gravity vector, \(S\) the actuator selection matrix, and \(A(q)^T\lambda\) represents the constraint reaction forces.

Equation (15):

\[ A(q)\dot{q} = 0 \]

Enforcing the closed-chain kinematic constraints that couple the crane and manipulator motions.

These equations form the foundation of the control design and are used within the ERG framework to ensure that all joint, torque, and motion constraints are satisfied during cooperative manipulation.
